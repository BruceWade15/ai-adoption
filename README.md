# Adoption of AI into Small Businesses

A short assessment by [M37 bot Factory](https://www.m37.co.za) for small and medium business owners. It measures the practical and emotional hurdles that hold businesses back from using AI to automate manual work.

- 10 questions, about 3 minutes
- Answers are scored on a 1 to 5 slider, for a total of 10 to 50
- Three result bands, each with a next step

Everything runs in the browser in one file, `index.html`. Names, emails and answers are not saved or sent anywhere.
